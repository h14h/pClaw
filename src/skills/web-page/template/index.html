The pick

The Jonsbo N3 at $150. It's the quietest of the three in measured reviews, has room to grow to 8 drives, and fits a mini-ITX board. The one catch is that it only takes an SFX power supply, which adds about $90 if you don't have one.

Options

  • Jonsbo N3 pick $149.99

    Amazon, in stock. Two 100 mm fans on the drive cage; swap for Noctua NF-A10s if the hum bothers you.

  • Fractal Node 304 cheapest $109.99

    Newegg, in stock. Older design, proven, near silent on the low fan setting.

  • Sliger CX3701 $239.00

    Direct from Sliger, ships in 2–3 weeks. Best drive cooling of the three and the cleanest look.

Caveats

Not verified: the Sliger lead time is from the product page, not a confirmed order. Prices change often; the figures above were seen on 2 Oct 2026.

Noise depends more on the fans and drives you put in than on the case. All three get quiet with a fan swap; the difference is how far you have to go.

How the noise numbers were gathered

ServeTheHome and TechPowerUp measure at one metre with the stock fans at idle. Level1Techs gave a subjective description only, so the Sliger has no figure.
